Why Breakfast Matters

A balanced breakfast can help provide energy and include nutritious foods in your daily diet.

1. Moong Dal Chilla

A protein-rich breakfast option that can be prepared with vegetables.

2. Idli with Sambar

Pairing idli with vegetable-rich sambar can make a more balanced meal.

3. Vegetable Oats

Oats with vegetables can provide fibre and other nutrients.

4. Vegetable Poha

Add vegetables and keep the portion appropriate for your meal plan.

5. Vegetable Upma

A vegetable-based breakfast option that can be included as part of a balanced diet.
